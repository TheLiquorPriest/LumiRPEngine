---
description: The plain line the chat shows the player when a turn ends without a reply, one line for each reason, a name, a colon, then its text. Where thinking had already gone out, the message the chat keeps shows the same line in its thinking, filled with no detail (the provider's or the error's own words, which go to the player's notice alone), since what is kept in the thinking can reach a later prompt; so a line reads whole without it. The lines whose names end `-after` say why a turn ended once its reply had begun to go out, so the reply stands and the turn's record of it was not finished; they are shown under that reply in the chat, beside the press that settles it, and said in RPE's log. The provider lines are given, where the failed call was made more than once, how many tries were made (`tries`) and what broke the last (`is.connection`, `is.timeout`, `is.overloaded`, `is.server`, `is.other`, `is.credit`, `is.key`, `is.invalid`, `is.tooLong`, `is.unmade`). The lines whose names begin `pass-` are for a request the chat application sent to RPE's connection to be passed on to a model, and that has no answer, because no connection could pass it on, because something in it cannot be carried by that connection, because the provider failed, or because it was stopped. Change the words freely. Delete a line and that reason is shown with no words.
format: lines
