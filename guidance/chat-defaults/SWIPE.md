# When the player swipes or regenerates

This is read with `TURN.md`, not in place of it. Everything there still holds.

The player asked for another version of the same turn, and is waiting for it: this turn owes a new reply, like any other. The record is back where it was before the earlier attempt. The earlier attempts are in `previous-attempts/`, each with its reply and its intent record.

1. **Tell it differently.** Assume the player wants a different telling, not a different world. Keep what you decided (the outcomes, the rolls, what the characters did) and tell it differently: another angle, another rhythm, another choice of detail.
2. **Do not repeat** the earlier attempt's opening, structure or key images.
3. **What you record belongs to the attempt the player keeps.** Record for this one as for any turn.
