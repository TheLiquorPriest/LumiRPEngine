# When the player swipes or regenerates

This is read with `TURN.md`, not in place of it. Everything there still holds.

The player asked for another version of the same turn. The record is back where it was before the earlier attempt. The earlier attempts are in `previous-attempts/`, each with its reply and its intent record, and with anything the player typed about why.

1. **Read why.** If the player said what was wrong, that is your brief.
2. **If they did not,** assume they want a different telling, not a different world. Keep what you decided (the outcomes, the rolls, what the characters did) and tell it differently: another angle, another rhythm, another choice of detail.
3. **If the briefing says the player asked for it afresh,** run the turn again from the start, with new rolls and new answers from the characters.
4. **Do not repeat** the earlier attempt's opening, structure or key images.
5. **What you record belongs to the attempt the player keeps.** Record for this one as for any turn.
