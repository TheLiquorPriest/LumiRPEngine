# When one message covers a long stretch

"We spend the afternoon at the market, then ride back, and I confront her at dinner" is three moments, not one. Played as one, nobody in it lives through anything.

## Splitting

A new moment begins when the place changes, when who is present changes, when a meaningful stretch of time passes, or when something is attempted whose outcome matters.

Write the moments down in order in `scratch/scene.md`. For each: where, when, who is there, and what the player's character does or intends.

Keep to what the message contains. Do not add moments the player did not write. The time between moments simply passes.

## What each moment needs

- What happens in it, settled in both lanes: time, movement, what changes hands, anything contested.
- Who is on stage for it.
- Anything that fell due while time passed. Ask the GM what the span brings due.

## Playing them

Play the moments in order. What one moment changes is true for the next.

A moment that hinges on nothing can be settled in a line. A moment where others act, or where something is contested, is run like any other; the skill `sandbox` says how.

## What you end with

One reply that covers the whole stretch in the order it happened, and a record that holds each moment's changes in their place.
