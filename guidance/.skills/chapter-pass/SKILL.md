# Closing a chapter

A chapter's summary stands for its scenes once even their summaries are too many to show. This pass is also the one time the story is read whole.

## 1. The summary

Read the chapter's scene summaries in order. Write the chapter's summary with `story_record`: ten to twenty lines.

- The chapter's shape: where it began, what turned, where it ended.
- Every fact later chapters stand on: who allied with whom, who knows what, what was won or lost, promises made.
- What is still open at its end.

Where a scene's summary is thin, read that scene's beats before you write, and its messages with `chat_read` if the beats are thin too.

## 2. The reread

Read the timeline since the last chapter against three things.

- **The premise.** Has the story drifted from what it is? If the player took it there, the premise has changed: record that. If you took it there, say so in your record of this pass.
- **The arcs.** Does each arc still describe where the plot and each principal stand? Rewrite the ones that do not.
- **The player model.** What did the player lean into in this chapter, and what did they steer away from? Add what you learned.

## 3. Corrections

Where the record says something the prose does not bear out (a beat that did not happen as written, a thread marked closed that is still open), correct it. Note each correction as a retcon, with its reason.

## What you end with

A chapter with its summary, and arcs and the player model brought up to date, with a short note of what you corrected. Nothing here changes what happened. It changes only the record of it.
