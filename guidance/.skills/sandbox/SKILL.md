description: Run a moment with the characters and the GM. Stage it, let each act on what they perceive, resolve, repeat. For turns that hinge on several minds, on a contest, or on a stretch of time with consequences.

# Running a moment at the table

1. **Write the scene sheet** to `scratch/scene.md`:
   - where and when;
   - who is on stage, where they stand, what state they are in;
   - what the player's character does, in order. This is binding. Do not change it;
   - what is open;
   - the lore in play, cited by entry;
   - the moments, if the player's message covers a stretch.

2. **For each moment:**
   - Give each acting principal the moment as that character perceives it, and call their agents together with `delegate`. Each already has who it is, what comes to mind, where it is, what it wears and carries, and the lore it would know. Add what it could perceive of the moment and nothing it could not. (What to record of that is in the perceptions rule.)
   - Give the GM its questions in the same breath if they do not depend on the characters' answers. Otherwise ask after.
   - Read what came back. If someone asked you something, answer it with `agent_message` before going on.
   - Resolve the moment into beats: what happened, in order, as true.
   - Go on to the next moment, or close.

3. **Voice supporting characters and extras yourself,** from their records.

4. **Never call an agent for the player's character.**

5. **You end with** the beats in order and the staged changes in both lanes. Then write the reply.

Keep it tight. One round per moment is usual. A second round is for when one character's answer changes what another would do.
