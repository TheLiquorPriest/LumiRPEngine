# Casting

A character's record is what every agent will know them by. Make it from what the player already wrote. Do not invent a person.

## Making the record

1. **Find the sources:** the character card, the persona, and the lore entries that define this character. Bind them with `cast_update`, and say which entry defines which side of them (background, appearance, voice).
2. **Draft the base** from those sources, in four short parts:
   - Seed: who they are, in two lines.
   - Soul: what drives them, and what they will not do.
   - Inner Voice: how they think, and how they talk to themselves.
   - Background: where they come from.
   Use the sources' own words where you can. Where the sources are silent, leave the part short. A gap in a person is a gap like any other, and the consent rule applies to it.
3. **Give them their game side** if the story needs it: where they are, what they wear and carry. Ask the GM when it turns on the rules.

## How large a part

- **Principal:** someone the story follows. They get their own agent and their own memories. Each member of a group chat is a principal. So is anyone the player keeps coming back to.
- **Supporting:** someone who matters to a scene or a thread, and whose inner life the story does not need yet. What they perceive is kept in full, so nothing is lost if they grow.
- **Extra:** a name and a line. A barkeep, a guard at a gate. Nothing is kept for them.

Cast by the part they play in the story, not by how much the lore says about them. When unsure, cast low: recasting upward loses nothing.

If the player pinned a casting, it stands.

## When a part grows

A supporting character starts to matter when they return, when the player seeks them out, or when a thread hangs on them. Recast them as a principal then. What they perceived so far goes to their new agent as perceptions not yet taken in, so their first memories are written by themselves, in their own voice.

## What you end with

A record with its sources bound, a base, a casting, and a place in the world. Say in one line what you cast them as, and why.
