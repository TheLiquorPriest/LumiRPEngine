Proposes world data for the player to review in Build. Nothing is written to the world: the player applies the proposals they want.

Each call is checked at once against the world's draft as it stands, and what stands is kept with the job, so a job cut short keeps what it proposed. A later proposal of the same thing stands over the earlier one: of the same collection and key; of the same entry title; of the same owner, activity, place, times and days for a routine or schedule. Sending a thing again, mended, is how what an answer names is put right.

The answer is a line, then data. For proposals and entries: read, how many items of this call could be read; standing, how many of the job's stand now; dropped, each reason something was dropped, with how many. For a map: whether it reads as a map, then its errors and its warnings, each with where in the map it is. For routines and schedules: read, and toFix, what is wrong with each item, named by its place in the call, its owner and its label; or problem, why nothing in the call could be read.
