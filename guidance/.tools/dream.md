Lets time settle: every memory not yet settled by a dream settles (what was short term becomes long term), and the weak ones among them let go of part of their strength at once, as does each memory you name to fade; the words stay, and time goes on fading them. The memories you name to keep weigh more, and the connections you name are kept. The answer lists what settled, what was kept, what faded and how each memory touched now stands. A dream leaves top of mind as it is.
