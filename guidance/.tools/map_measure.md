---
description: map_measure as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  at: "The place whose map to measure on; leave it out when from names someone or something on a map."
  from: "Where to measure from: someone or something on the map, or a square [column, row]."
  to: "Where to measure to, the same way: gives the distance, whether the two see each other, and the way between with its cost and time."
  within: "A range in squares: who and what stands within it of from."
  area: "An area that starts at from: burst (all round it), cone or line."
  size: "The area's size in squares: a burst's radius, a cone's or a line's length."
  toward: "Where a cone or a line points: someone or something on the map, or a square."
  speed: "Squares the mover covers in a round, for the way's time; the map's own when left out."
---
Measures on a place's map of squares as play stands now: the distance between two spots in squares and feet; whether they see each other (walls, doors that are not open and whatever blocks the view stop it); the way between them, what it costs and how long it takes; who and what stands within a range; and the squares a burst, a cone or a line covers, with who stands in it. Someone who stands on no square is said to, with where they are kept (a zone or words), and the rest is measured. It changes nothing.
