description: How the tools for groups and organizations are called - gm_org and orgs_who_may. It says how to call them, not when. Delete this file and the agents that are given it by a search are given no such guide.
---
gm_org makes one change to a group in each call, in `change`: founding it, admitting or removing a member, giving a position or a title, making or taking away a position, delegating or revoking a power. orgs_who_may reads and changes nothing: give the `org` and the `power`, and with a `who` it says whether that person may use it. Each tool that writes also takes `source`, `precision` and `standing`.

    orgs_who_may {"org": "the Harbour Guild", "power": "set tolls", "who": "Ada"}
