---
description: map_view as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  at: "The place whose map to show, or someone or something standing on it."
  centre: "For a map too large to show whole, what to show around: a square [column, row], or someone or something on the map."
---
Shows a place's map of squares as text: the picture, its legend, who and what stands on which square ([column, row], counted from 1 at the top left), who is there with no square yet, and each door, open, closed or locked. A map too large to show whole is shown around a centre. It changes nothing.
