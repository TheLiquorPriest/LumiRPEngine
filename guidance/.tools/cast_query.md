---
description: cast_query as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  who: "The character, by name or id. Leave it out for who is in the cast, and as what; with part knowledge, for who knows what the words or the secret name."
  part: "Which part: sources, base, casting, growth, mind, relationships, knowledge, history, perceptions, or away (where a principal out of view is and what it means to do). Leave it out for every part."
  with: "relationships: only toward this person."
  words: "Only entries that hold all of these words."
  secret: "knowledge: only what is known of this secret, by the id the lore map shows it with (e-112, e-112#2, w-31)."
  history: "true: every version of the base, and each entry with every write of it, oldest first; struck entries too."
  skip: "Skip this many entries of each part first, to see the rest of a long one."
---
Reads a character's record as it stands on this turn's path. Sources are read from Lumiverse as they are now; one marked newer than the base has changed since the base was drawn from it. The base comes with the growth the Author accepted laid over it. Each entry comes with its id, its date in story time, and since and touched: the messages of the turns that first and last wrote it (empty for this turn, whose reply is not saved yet). Knowledge is what the character was given in perceptions and by the GM (kept_in cast) and what the minds' own record holds for the person it is, the secrets it holds among them, with how each was learned, who told it, whether it is believed and how sure they are (held, kept_in minds). With no character named and knowledge asked for, by words or by a secret's id, it says who in the cast knows it and how each came to.
