---
description: world_query as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  at: "The point of the tree: a place, someone or something (by name or id), world, groups or elsewhere; or ruleset for the world's ruleset and its bestiary."
  depth: "How many levels below the point to show, or full for everything beneath it."
  due: "A span ahead (two hours, until 22:00, until a part of the world's day by its name, or a date and time YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM): what falls due in it at that part of the tree."
  map: "true to add the place's map as text."
  part: "For a place: since, feature or access, to read that part of its record whole (what the story changed there, its features, who may enter and how it stands) in place of the newest few the place's lines show."
---
Reads the world's tree at a point: the point with its values and where each comes from, the levels beneath it to the depth asked, each with a line on what is beneath it. At a place opened in full it also gives the place's record, a line for each entry: its base and the lore entries it comes from, its ways to other places with the minutes and distance by each way of travel, what adjoins it, and the newest few entries of each of what the story changed there (since), its features and its access, with a line saying how many more there are and the query that reads them; a part with nothing in it says so. Asked for a span, it also lists what falls due in that span there; it applies none of it.
