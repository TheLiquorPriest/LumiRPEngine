Applies operations to the world's recorded state in order, each on the state the ones before it left, and answers with each one's lane, what changed, how things now stand, and what does not add up. A name that is not there, or a value that is none of the choices, answers the whole call with what would work and records nothing; but a thing that move, place, give, wear or store names with no recorded name near it is made where the one who acts is. Words that name nothing are kept as your words. A thing spawned at, in or on words that name nothing is made where its owner is, else at the scene's place, as assumed; with neither, the call is refused. act applies the outcome's effects as the action defines them, sets the activity it starts with its end and meets what that activity meets at that end; a requirement of the action that does not hold is said, and the action is carried out all the same. An act under the world's tabletop rules answers with what the rules worked out, each number with its parts; one taken by a fighter in a fight under way is recorded into the fight and spends what it costs of their turn. Its own time is given in the answer and does not pass: pass_time passes it. An act the call cannot carry out is answered with what its action takes and a call with a value of each.
