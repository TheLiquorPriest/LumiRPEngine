Applies a list of operations to the world's recorded state, in order, each on the state the ones before it left, and answers with each one's lane, what changed, how each touched thing now stands, and reports of what does not add up. A name that is not there, or a value that is none of the choices, answers the whole call with what would work and records nothing; but a thing that move, place, give, wear or store names with no recorded name near it is made where the one who acts is. Words that name nothing are kept as your words (a horizon entry, where a thing was said to be). A thing spawned at, in or on words that name nothing is made where its owner is, else at the scene's place, and recorded as assumed; with neither, the call is refused.
