description: recall as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  cue: "A person, place, thing, topic or feeling, in a few words."
  skip: "Skip this many first, to bring back the rest."
  top: "true to put what comes back at top of mind."
---
Brings back your memories that touch the cue: those about it, and those whose words hold it, strongest first. Recalling a memory strengthens it, so it comes back in full even if it had faded. Each comes with its id, its tier (top of mind, short term or long term) and its date in story time. Recalling with top puts what comes back at top of mind: where that passes the limit, the weakest there leave it (they stay memories), and the answer names them.
