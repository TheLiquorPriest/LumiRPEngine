---
description: recall as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  cue: "A person, place, thing, topic or feeling, in a few words."
  skip: "Skip this many first, to bring back the rest."
---
Brings back your memories that touch the cue: those about it, and those whose words hold it, strongest first. Recalling a memory strengthens it, so it comes back in full even if it had faded. Each comes with its id, its tier (top of mind, short term or long term) and its date in story time.
