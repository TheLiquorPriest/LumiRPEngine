---
description: roll as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
doing: rolling the dice
arguments:
  who: "Whose roll it is."
  for: "What the roll decides, kept with it."
  dice: "Dice to roll, such as 1d20+3, 2d6 or 4d6kh3."
  check: "One of the world's checks, rolled with who's sheet."
  against: "A difficulty the total must reach."
  target: "Who a check is rolled against, by name or id: they roll against it from their own sheet. A difficulty given in against is reached instead."
  sum: "A sum to work out, with + - * / and brackets, such as 12.50 * 3 * 1.08. With dice or check it is kept with the roll; alone it is only answered."
---
Rolls once for someone, by dice or by one of the world's checks, and records the roll with what it was for. A check against someone is rolled against the target, who rolls their side. The answer gives the dice kept, the total, the natural die, the difficulty, the outcome, and the roll's id, which a later write can cite as its source.
