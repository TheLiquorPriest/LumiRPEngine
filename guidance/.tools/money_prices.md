description: money_prices as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  item: "The thing to price, by its catalog id, its price category, or in words."
  place: "Where; left out, where the player's character is."
---
Prices a thing at a place: the price of one, its range there from cheap to dear, and whether the place sells it. With no thing named, lists everything the place sells, each at its price. It changes nothing.
