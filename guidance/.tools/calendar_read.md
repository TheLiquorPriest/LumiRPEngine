  holiday: "A holiday or named day, by name; left out, all of them."

Reads the calendar's holidays and named days as this chat keeps them: the world's own, less those taken off in play, with those changed or added. Each is given with its rule, whether it is a day off, and the day it next falls on. Nothing is changed.
