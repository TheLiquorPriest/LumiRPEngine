---
description: remember as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  memories: The memories, written in order, in one write.
  memories.id: "A memory of yours to change, by its id. Leave it out to make a new one."
  memories.text: "What happened, as you lived it, in your own words."
  memories.gist: "The same in one line. When the memory fades, this is all that is left."
  memories.weight: "How much it matters to you, from 1 (an ordinary moment) up. The heavier, the slower it fades."
  memories.tone: "How it felt."
  memories.who: "The people it is about, by name."
  memories.about: "The places, things and topics it is about."
  memories.from: "The ids of the perceptions it comes from. Each one named is taken in."
  memories.taken: "The ids of perceptions that are taken in with nothing to remember of them: send only this, with no text, and they are not given to you again."
  memories.top: "true to carry it at the top of your mind; false to set it down."
---
Commits memories of yours, as many as you need in one call. A memory belongs to this turn: if the turn is swiped or deleted, it goes. The answer gives each memory's id and how it now stands.
