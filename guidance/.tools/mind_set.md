---
description: mind_set as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  entries: What is on your mind, written in order, in one write.
  entries.id: "An entry of yours to change, by its id. Leave it out to add one."
  entries.kind: "attention, feeling, belief, want, plan or hiding (what you hide)."
  entries.text: "What it is, in your own words."
  entries.about: "The people, places, things and topics it is about."
  entries.from: "The ids of the perceptions it comes from."
  entries.drop: "true when it is no longer on your mind; false to bring it back."
  entries.why: "Why it changed or went."
---
Sets what is on your mind now: what holds your attention, what you feel, what you believe, what you want, what you plan, and what you hide. As many entries as you need in one call. An entry belongs to this turn: if the turn is swiped or deleted, it goes. The answer gives each entry's id and how it now stands.
