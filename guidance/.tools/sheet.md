Reads someone's sheet under the world's tabletop rules, with every total's parts and where each part comes from: abilities and their modifiers, defence and its touch and flat-footed variants, initiative, each weapon in hand and each attack of their own (the attack, the damage, the threat and what a critical multiplies, the range, the rounds loaded), saves, skills, hit points (temporary hit points and nonlethal harm with them), the conditions and effects on them with what is left of each, daily gauges, classes and traits. It changes nothing. A world without such rules has no sheet to read here, and the answer says so.
