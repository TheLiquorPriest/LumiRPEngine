---
description: reflect as the model is told it - what the tool does, and what each of its arguments is.
arguments:
  from: "The ids of the memories it comes from."
  text: "The realization, in your own words."
  gist: "The same in one line."
  weight: "How much it matters to you, from 1 up."
  tone: "How it feels."
  who: "The people it is about, by name."
  about: "The places, things and topics it is about."
  growth: "How it changes you, if it does, in one line. The Author decides whether it stands."
  top: "true to put the realization at top of mind."
---
Turns several of your memories into a realization, kept as a memory of its own with the memories it came from. The answer gives its id, and the id of the change it proposes if it proposes one. If it changes how you stand with someone or what is on your mind, say so with relate or mind_set and name the realization in from.
